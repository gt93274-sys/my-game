📖 Your field guide

Move between three lanes. Jump over broken stone barriers. Slide under hanging traps. At marked intersections, turn in the indicated direction. Stay alive long enough to escape the temple.

🗺️ Temple expedition

Finish a level to unlock the next. At a randomized point during a run, choose whether to cross a portal into a dark mirror-world or stay on the bright temple path. Endless runs also feature recurring portals; each dark-world visit ends after a short distance. Levels 3, 6, 9, and every third level after that grant a collectible seal.

🧭 Field notes

The temple rewards rhythm more than reckless speed. Read the ground, make a plan, and treat every junction as a decision.

🛣️
Three lanes, one safe route

Lane changes move gradually. Aim for a clear lane before an obstacle reaches the foreground; a last-moment switch may not finish in time.

🪨
Read each hazard

Jump broken barriers and gaps. Slide under the hanging DUCK signs. Crates block the path, so change lanes or jump over them.

↪️
Junction warnings are timed

When a turn warning appears, tap TURN or swipe toward the arrow before the junction passes. A successful turn adds distance.

🛡️
Power-ups are temporary

Shields absorb a collision, magnets pull nearby bananas toward your lane, and boosts raise your speed. Their active state is shown in the HUD.

👹
Collisions raise the danger

A hit brings the chaser closer. Brief grace time prevents repeated damage from one mistake, but consecutive hazards can end the expedition.

🏵️
Seal levels mark milestones

Every third temple level awards a seal when completed. Rare seals may also appear on the road and add bonus bananas.
